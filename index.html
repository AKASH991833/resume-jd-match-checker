      How it works

      1. It finds skills and tools from a built-in list of about 300 IT and general skills, with short forms like k8s or js.
      2. It also lists other words the job repeats a lot.
      3. Score = how much of the job's keywords (weighted by how often and how strongly they appear) show up in your resume.

      Limits: lite version. It reads plain text only, does not understand synonyms beyond its list, and cannot judge how good your experience is. Real ATS tools differ. A high score does not guarantee a call, and a low score does not mean you should not apply.
